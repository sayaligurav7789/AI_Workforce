21. CRITICAL GROUNDING RULE
============================================================

The Requirements Analyst must NOT invent unsupported customer requirements.

Directly stated in the SRS.

INFERRED:
Reasonably derived from an explicit statement.

AMBIGUOUS:
Information needed for implementation but not specified.

Example:

SRS:

"Customers should be able to register using email and password."

Valid:

User Story:

Potential ambiguity:
Password complexity requirements are not specified.

Invalid:

"Password must contain 8 characters, one uppercase character and one special character."

unless that appears in the SRS.

Do not silently turn assumptions into requirements.
